    2. How to Access
    Open from any phone, tablet, or computer
    1
    Open your browser
    Use Safari (iPhone/iPad), Chrome (Android), or any browser on a computer.
    2
    Go to the Notice Board URL
    Type or tap the address provided by the Technical Office:
    kcnprhospital-web.github.io/communication-channel/
    3
    Save to your home screen (recommended)
    iPhone: Tap the Share button (□↑) → "Add to Home Screen" → Add
    Android: Tap ⋮ menu → "Add to Home Screen"
    This makes it open like an app with one tap.
    💡
    Tip: The Notice Board also sends messages to your hospital Telegram group. You can tap the link in any Telegram notification to open the relevant notice directly.

    3. How to Sign In
    Use your Staff ID and password
    1
    Enter your Staff ID
    Your Staff ID is a code like S006 — issued by the Technical Office. It starts with the letter S followed by numbers.
    2

    You will be taken to the Notice Board immediately. No internet required for sign-in — it works offline.

    Forgot your credentials? Contact the Technical Office (Dr. Yeap Kong) or Administration (Mr. Chheng Sokhim) to reset your password.

    4. Reading Notices
    Browse and filter official communications
    1
    Browse the notice list
    All notices are shown on the main screen, newest first. Each notice shows the title, category, author, date, and your acknowledgment status.
    2
    Understand the color labels
    🚨 URGENT 📅 MEETING 📄 POLICY 🎓 TRAINING ℹ️ INFO
    3
    Filter notices
    Use the filter chips (All, Urgent, Meeting, Policy, Training, Info) at the top to show only what you need. On mobile, use the bottom tab bar to switch between All Notices, Pending ACK, and Meetings.
    4
    Tap any notice to read it
    Tapping a notice opens the full content — including the full body text, event date, location, attachment reference, and any action buttons.
    ℹ️
    Notices marked "NEW" (red badge) have not been read yet. The Unread counter in the sidebar shows how many new notices await you.

    5. How to Acknowledge a Notice
    Confirm you have read and understood
    ❗
    Acknowledgment is mandatory for notices marked with "⏳ ACK Required". Failure to acknowledge by the deadline will be recorded and reported to your department head.

    ✅ 3 Ways to Acknowledge

    A
    In the Notice Board app (main method)
    1. Open the Notice Board → sign in
    2. Tap the notice that requires acknowledgment
    3. Scroll down to the "⚠️ Acknowledgment Required" box
    4. Tap the green button: "✅ I Acknowledge This Notice"
    5. Done — your name and timestamp are recorded permanently.
    B
    From the Pending ACK tab (fastest)
    1. On mobile, tap ⏳ Pending in the bottom tab bar
    2. All notices needing your acknowledgment are listed here
    3. Tap each one → tap the green acknowledge button
    This is the fastest way to clear all pending acknowledgments at once.
    C
    From the Telegram notification link
    1. When a new notice is posted, your hospital Telegram group receives a notification
    2. Tap "Open Notice Board" link in the Telegram message
    3. Sign in → the notice opens automatically
    4. Tap the green acknowledge button
    💡
    Check your status: Look at the bottom of each notice card. ✅ Acknowledged means done. ⏳ ACK Required means you still need to confirm.
    📅
    6. Meeting Invitations & Attendance
    RSVP and check in for meetings

    When a meeting invitation is posted with attendance tracking enabled, you will see two additional sections inside the notice: RSVP and Check-In.

    1
    RSVP — Confirm your attendance in advance
    Open the meeting notice → scroll to the "📊 Meeting Attendance" section.

    If you will attend: Tap ✋ Yes, Attending
    If you cannot attend: Tap ❌ Send Apology → you may type a reason (e.g. "On duty", "Emergency coverage")
    2
    Check In — Record your physical arrival
    When you arrive at the meeting room, open the Notice Board → tap the meeting notice → tap "📍 Check In Now".

    Your name and exact arrival time are recorded. This is separate from RSVP — you must still check in even if you RSVP'd "Yes".
    ⚠️
    Important: If you cannot attend, please send an apology as early as possible so the meeting organizer can plan accordingly. Last-minute absences without apology will be noted in the attendance record.
    🔔
    7. Receiving Notifications
    Stay informed automatically
    1
    Telegram group notifications (automatic)
    Every time a new notice is posted, a message is sent to the hospital Telegram group. You will see the notice title, date, location, and a link to open it. Make sure you are a member of the hospital Telegram group.
    2
    In-app notification bell
    The 🔔 bell icon in the top-right corner shows a red badge when new notices arrive. Tap it to see recent notifications and jump directly to the notice.
    3
    Reminder notifications
    If you have not acknowledged a notice before the deadline, admin can send a reminder. You will receive another Telegram message with a direct link to acknowledge.

    8. Admin Guide — Posting Notices
    For Directors, Deputy Directors, Department Heads, and Technical Office

    How to post a new notice

    1
    Sign in with an Admin account
    Admin accounts (S001–S007) show additional options: a sidebar "Admin" section and a blue "✏️ Post" button in the top right.
    2
    Tap "✏️ Post" or "Post Notice" in the sidebar
    A form will open from the bottom of the screen.
    3
    Fill in the notice details
    Title * — Clear, descriptive title
    Category * — Meeting / Policy / Urgent / Training / Info
    Department — Target specific dept or "All Departments"
    Content * — Full notice text (supports Khmer and English)
    Date & Time — For meetings and events
    Location — Room or venue
    Attachment — Reference filename (e.g. Agenda-May2026.pdf)
    Attendance Tracking — For meetings: enable RSVP and check-in
    Require Acknowledgment — Staff must confirm they read it
    ACK Deadline — Date by which all staff must acknowledge
    4
    Tap "📢 Post & Notify"
    The notice is posted immediately and a Telegram notification is sent automatically to the hospital group. Staff will see it in the app the next time they refresh.

    How to track acknowledgments

    1
    Open any notice you posted
    Tap the notice → scroll down to see the "✅ Acknowledgment Tracker" section showing confirmed vs pending counts.
    2
    Send a reminder to pending staff
    Tap "📨 Remind Pending (N)" button. A Telegram message is sent to the hospital group reminding staff who have not yet acknowledged.
    ℹ️
    Adding new staff: Go to Sidebar → "👥 Add Staff" → fill in the name, department, role, email, and password. The staff member can then sign in immediately with their new credentials.

    10. Frequently Asked Questions
    Common questions and solutions
    ❓ I can't sign in — the button does nothing
    Make sure you are entering your Staff ID exactly (e.g. S006 with capital S) and password correctly. If it still doesn't work, try refreshing the page. Contact Technical Office to verify your credentials.
    ❓ I don't see any notices
    Tap the 🔄 refresh button (top right) to load the latest notices from the server. If you are offline, demo notices will be shown.
    ❓ I acknowledged a notice but my status still shows "⏳ ACK Required"
    Tap the 🔄 refresh button to reload. The acknowledgment is recorded immediately but the display sometimes needs a refresh to update.
    ❓ I am not receiving Telegram notifications
    Make sure you are a member of the hospital Telegram group (Hôpital Provinciale de Kampongchhnang). Notifications are sent to the group, not to individual users. Contact admin if you have been removed from the group.
    ❓ Can I use this on my phone?
    Yes. The app works on any smartphone browser. For the best experience, add it to your home screen so it opens like an app. See Section 2 for instructions.
    ❓ Who do I contact for help?
    Technical issues: Technical Office — Dr. Yeap Kong (S006)
    Login problems: Administration — Mr. Chheng Sokhim (S007)
    Notice content: Contact the notice author directly
